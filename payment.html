Payment Help

Payment Failed?

If your payment fails, first check your bank or UPI app for the transaction status. Avoid paying again until you confirm whether the first payment was successful.

Money Deducted but Order Not Confirmed?

Keep your transaction ID and payment receipt. Contact our support team with your order details so the payment can be checked.

Refund Information

If a refund is approved, it will be processed according to the applicable refund policy and payment provider's processing time.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q1. Is online payment safe?

Payments should be completed only through the official checkout and its supported payment gateway.

Q2. What should I do if money is deducted twice?

Keep both transaction references and contact support for verification.

Q3. How can I check my payment status?

Check your order confirmation and transaction status in your payment app or bank account.

Q4. Where can I ask for payment help?

Visit our Contact page and share your order ID and payment reference. Never share your OTP, PIN or password.
