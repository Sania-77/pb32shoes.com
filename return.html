Return & Exchange Policy

1. Product Condition

Products should be unused and returned with original packaging, tags and accessories, as applicable under the final store policy.

2. Size Exchange

Customers can contact support for a size exchange request. The requested size will depend on stock availability.

3. Damaged or Wrong Product

If you receive a damaged or incorrect item, contact support with your order ID and clear photos for assistance.

4. Refund Process

Approved refunds will be processed according to the final refund policy and the payment provider's processing time.
