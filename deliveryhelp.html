Delivery Information

1. Order Processing

Your order will be processed after successful confirmation. Processing time depends on product availability and business operations.

2. Shipping Updates

Once your order is dispatched, tracking details can be shared through the available order communication channel.

3. Delivery Delays

Delivery may be delayed due to courier operations, weather, holidays or location-related issues.

4. Wrong Delivery Address

Contact support as soon as possible if your address needs correction. Changes may not be possible after dispatch.

5. Missing Parcel

If your tracking shows delivered but you have not received your parcel, contact support with your Order ID.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q1. How can I track my delivery?

Enter your Order ID above. Live tracking will work once connected to an order management system.

Q2. What if my delivery is delayed?

Check your latest tracking update and contact support if you need further assistance.

Q3. Can I change my delivery address?

Contact support immediately. Address changes depend on the order's shipping status.

Q4. What if my parcel is marked delivered but I haven't received it?

Check with household members or your building's reception, then contact our support team with your Order ID.
